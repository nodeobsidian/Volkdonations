How Campaigns Work

From Donation to Delivery

Every campaign is scoped, budgeted, and tracked before it's published — so you always know exactly what your donation funds.

1
A Need Is Identified

Field teams and local partners document a specific, fundable need — a well, a surgery fund, a classroom rebuild.

2
A Campaign Is Scoped

We set a transparent budget and goal amount, published in full before the campaign goes live for donations.

3
Donors Fund It Together

Every gift, large or small, moves the progress bar. Donors can track funding in real time on this page.

4
Impact Is Reported Back

Once complete, we publish outcomes and photos so every donor sees exactly what their support delivered.
