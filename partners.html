Strategic Partners

Long-standing institutional partnerships that shape our programs at scale

6 Partners
BF

Bahari Children's Foundation

Nairobi, Kenya  ·  Est. 2004
Child Welfare Education East Africa

One of East Africa's most established child welfare organizations, Bahari operates 14 care centers across Kenya and Tanzania, providing residential support, schooling, and vocational training to over 2,400 orphaned children. Our partnership funds their secondary education scholarship program and nutrition initiative.

Partner since 2011 Strategic
SE

Solaris Education Trust

Mumbai, India  ·  Est. 2001
Education Access Healthcare South Asia

Solaris operates an award-winning network of 31 community learning centers across Maharashtra and Gujarat, reaching children in rural and peri-urban communities who would otherwise have no access to formal schooling. Volk Donations co-funds their digital literacy labs and mobile health clinics.

Partner since 2013 Strategic
MF

Meridian Global Fund

Geneva, Switzerland  ·  Est. 1998
Grantmaking Research Global

A Geneva-based independent philanthropic fund that channels resources to vetted child welfare programs across 22 countries. Meridian co-grants with Volk Donations on programs in West Africa and Southeast Asia, providing funding, technical assistance, and impact measurement expertise.

Partner since 2015 Strategic
AU

Amaney Ubuntu Network

Johannesburg, South Africa  ·  Est. 2007
Community Care HIV/AIDS Support Southern Africa

The Ubuntu Network connects 47 community-based care groups across South Africa, Zambia, and Zimbabwe that support children orphaned by HIV/AIDS. Their community mother model — trained local women providing family-based care — has become a best-practice template adopted across the region.

Partner since 2012 Strategic
PI

Pacifico Infancia

Bogotá, Colombia  ·  Est. 2005
Child Protection Psychosocial Latin America

Pacifico Infancia operates crisis shelters, trauma counseling programs, and family reintegration services across Colombia, Ecuador, and Peru. Specializing in children displaced by conflict or family breakdown, their psychosocial model is recognized by UNICEF as a regional best practice.

Partner since 2016 Strategic
NT

Nile Tomorrow Initiative

Cairo, Egypt  ·  Est. 2009
Vocational Training Youth Employment North Africa

Focused on the critical 16–24 age group who age out of orphanage systems, Nile Tomorrow runs trade training centers in Cairo, Alexandria, and Aswan. Their graduates achieve 78% formal employment within 18 months — one of the highest rates in the region. Volk Donations funds their hospitality and technology tracks.

Partner since 2018 Strategic
