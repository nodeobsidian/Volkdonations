When you visit our Platform, we automatically collect certain technical data through cookies, server logs, and similar technologies:

  • Public Sources: Publicly available professional profiles, for major donor research purposes only (applicable to gifts above $10,000).

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For individuals in the EEA, United Kingdom, or jurisdictions with equivalent data protection laws, we process your personal data under the following GDPR legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to process your donation, manage your account, or provide services you have requested.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Processing required to comply with tax law, anti-money laundering regulations, charity law, and other applicable legal obligations.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing for fraud prevention, security, platform analytics, and communications with existing donors about program updates, where our interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Processing for marketing to non-donor individuals, or for any purposes where we have specifically sought your permission. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Our website enforces HTTPS across all pages.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data fields (including donor financial records) are encrypted at rest using AES-256 in our AWS-hosted infrastructure.
  • Payment Security (PCI-DSS Level 1): We do not store full payment card numbers. All payment processing is handled by Stripe, certified to PCI-DSS Level 1 — the highest level of payment security certification.
  • Access Controls: Access to personal data is restricted on a need-to-know basis, with role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication, and annual security training for all staff.
  • Vulnerability Management: We conduct annual penetration testing and quarterly vulnerability assessments of our Platform and infrastructure.
  • Incident Response: We maintain a documented Data Breach Response Plan. In the event of a breach, we will notify affected individuals and relevant authorities within 72 hours (GDPR) or as otherwise required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal information. To exercise any right, contact us at privacy@volkdonations.website or through your account settings. We respond within 30 days (or 45 days for complex requests).

Right of Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you, including its source, purpose, and recipients.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data where we no longer have a legal basis to retain it (the "right to be forgotten").

Right to Restrict Processing

Request that we limit how we use your personal data while a dispute about accuracy or lawfulness is resolved.

Right to Data Portability

Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format and transfer it to another controller.

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing, at any time.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for consent-based processing at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

File a complaint with your local data protection authority (ICO in the UK; your national DPA in the EU/EEA).

International Data Transfers

Volk Donations is headquartered in the United States. If you are located in the EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction outside the US, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA or UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses: We use the European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for transfers from the EEA, and ICO-approved International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) for transfers from the UK.
  • Adequacy Decisions: Where the European Commission has determined that a third country provides adequate protection, we may rely on that adequacy decision.
  • Binding Corporate Rules: For some service providers, we rely on approved Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) governing data protection across their global operations.

You may request a copy of our Standard Contractual Clauses by contacting privacy@volkdonations.website.

Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not directed to children under the age of 13 in the United States, or under the age of 16 in the EEA and UK. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below these ages without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@volkdonations.website. We will promptly delete such information upon verification.

In all cases, photographs and stories of beneficiary children published in our communications are produced with full consent of appropriate guardians in compliance with our Child Safeguarding Policy, available upon request.

California Residents — CCPA / CPRA Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grant you specific rights regarding your personal information, in addition to those set out in Section 9:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions under California law.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out is currently required, but you may submit a request to confirm this at any time.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights — including by denying services, charging different prices, or providing a different quality of service.

California "Shine the Light" (Civil Code § 1798.83): We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. California residents may contact us at privacy@volkdonations.website to confirm this at any time.

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at privacy@volkdonations.website with subject line "CCPA Request," or call +1 (720) 844-3920. We will verify your identity before processing and respond within 45 days.
