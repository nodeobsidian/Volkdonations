🚩 Common Scam Signs

What to watch out for

💸
Unusual Payment MethodsWire transfers, cryptocurrency, gift cards, or Western Union payments — we never request these.
🔗
Lookalike DomainsURLs like volk-donations.net, volkdonations.org.co, or volkdonatons.com are not us.
⏰
Urgent Pressure Tactics"Act now," countdown timers, or threats of missed emergencies are manipulation strategies.
📞
No Verifiable ContactLegitimate charities have registered addresses, phone numbers, and EINs. Ask for them.
📄
No Tax DocumentationWe are EIN 45-1234567. Scam sites cannot provide IRS tax receipts for U.S. tax deductions.
