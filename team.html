Careers

Work That Matters.

We hire people who are not looking for a job — they are looking for a cause. If you want your professional skills to move children's lives forward, we want to hear from you.

At Volk Donations, we believe that the quality of our people is the single greatest determinant of our impact. We hire across functions — from finance and technology to communications and field operations — and we invest seriously in our team's growth, wellbeing, and career development.

We are a remote-first organisation with staff in 22 time zones. We operate with radical trust, high accountability, and a genuine belief that diverse teams make better decisions.

🌍
Remote-First, Globally Distributed
Work from anywhere. We have staff in 22 countries and operate across time zones with asynchronous-first communication.
📈
Structured Career Development
Annual learning budgets, conference attendance, and a formal mentorship programme with senior leadership.
❤️
Competitive Compensation & Wellness
Sector-leading nonprofit salaries, comprehensive health coverage, and 28 days annual leave for all full-time staff.
